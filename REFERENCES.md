## 🏛️ 1. National Education Policies & Central Government Missions (Govt. of India)

1. **National Education Policy (NEP 2020) — Mother Tongue Education Mandate**  
   *Ministry of Education, Government of India*  
   *Reference Section 4.11 – 4.14:* Enforces mother tongue and regional tribal language as the primary medium of instruction up to Grade 5 to ensure foundational literacy.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://www.education.gov.in/nep/nep-2020](https://www.education.gov.in/nep/nep-2020)

2. **Bhashini Mission (National Language Translation Mission - NLTM)**  
   *Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India*  
   *National Vision:* Empowering Indian citizens through open-source language technology and AI for scheduled and tribal Indian languages.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://bhashini.gov.in/](https://bhashini.gov.in/)

3. **NIPUN Bharat Mission — Foundational Literacy & Numeracy (FLN) Guidelines**  
   *Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education, Govt. of India*  
   *Framework:* Guidelines for mother-tongue audio-visual learning tools in early primary grades (Class 1 to 3).  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://dsel.education.gov.in/nipun_bharat](https://dsel.education.gov.in/nipun_bharat)

4. **Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan (Integrated Scheme for School Education)**  
   *Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education, Govt. of India*  
   *School Infrastructure Mandate:* Provision of digital teaching aids and mother-tongue learning materials for rural and tribal schools.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://samagrashiksha.education.gov.in/](https://samagrashiksha.education.gov.in/)

## 🌾 2. State Government Frameworks & Jharkhand Education Bodies

5. **Department of School Education & Literacy — Govt. of Jharkhand**  
   *Government of Jharkhand*  
   *Field Baseline:* Directives for primary school mother-tongue education and Anganwadi tribal language transition in Jharkhand.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://schooleducation.jharkhand.gov.in/](https://schooleducation.jharkhand.gov.in/)

6. **Jharkhand Education Project Council (JEPC)**  
   *Department of School Education & Literacy, Govt. of Jharkhand*  
   *Implementation Body:* Operational guidelines for primary education in tribal districts of Santhal Pargana and Kolhan.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://jepc.jharkhand.gov.in/](https://jepc.jharkhand.gov.in/)

7. **Jharkhand Council of Educational Research and Training (JCERT)**  
   *Govt. of Jharkhand*  
   *Primary Textbooks:* Class 1–5 primary school textbooks, story cards, and FLN worksheets digitized for Bhasha Gyan offline access.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://jcert.jharkhand.gov.in/](https://jcert.jharkhand.gov.in/)

8. **Dr. Ramdayal Munda Tribal Welfare Research Institute (TRI), Ranchi**  
   *Department of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Minority & OBC Welfare, Govt. of Jharkhand*  
   *Tribal Language Documentation:* Research and documentation on Santali (Ol Chiki), Ho, Mundari, Kurukh, and Kharia language preservation.  
   🔗 **Official URL:** [https://trijharkhand.in/](https://trijharkhand.in/)

## 🇮🇳 3. Indian Academic Research Institutions & Premier Labs (IITs, CIIL, CDAC)

9. **AI4Bharat Research Lab — IIT Madras (IndicTrans2 Architecture)**  
   *Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras)*  
   *Research Paper:* "IndicTrans2: Towards High-Quality Multilingual Machine Translation for Indian Languages" (Gowda et al., 2023).  
   🔗 **Official Portal:** [https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/)  
   🔗 **IndicTrans2 Research:** [https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2)

10. **Central Institute of Indian Languages (CIIL), Mysore**  
    *Ministry of Education, Government of India*  
    *National Translation Mission (NTM) & Scheme for Protection and Preservation of Endangered Languages (SPPEL):* Standard linguistic dictionaries and orthography for Santali Ol Chiki (`ᱚᱞ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ`).  
    🔗 **Official URL:** [https://www.ciil.org/](https://www.ciil.org/)  
    🔗 **National Translation Mission:** [https://www.ntm.org.in/](https://www.ntm.org.in/)

11. **Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), Pune & Kolkata**  
    *Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India*  
    *GIST Group (Graphics & Intelligence Based Script Technology):* Indian language computing technologies, Unicode fonts, and keyboard layouts for Santali Ol Chiki script.  
    🔗 **Official URL:** [https://www.cdac.in/](https://www.cdac.in/)

12. **Department of Humanities & Social Sciences — IIT Kharagpur**  
    *Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur)*  
    *Linguistic Research:* Field studies on primary education language barriers and Santali script literacy in Eastern India.  
    🔗 **Official Portal:** [https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/)

## 📊 4. National Census & Demographic Data (Govt. of India)

13. **Census of India — Mother Tongue & Language Division**  
    *Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, Ministry of Home Affairs, Govt. of India*  
    *Demographic Baseline:* Official speaker statistics for Santali in Jharkhand districts (Dumka, Godda, Sahibganj, Pakur, Jamtara, Deoghar, East Singhbhum, West Singhbhum).  
    🔗 **Official URL:** [https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/data/census_tables](https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/data/census_tables)

14. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) — IS 16350: Indian Script Code for Information Interchange**  
    *Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Govt. of India*  
    *National Standard:* Indian national standardization guidelines for digital processing of Santali Ol Chiki script.  
    🔗 **Official URL:** [https://www.bis.gov.in/](https://www.bis.gov.in/)
